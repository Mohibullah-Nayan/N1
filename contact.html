Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about SCENT orders and boutique services.

How can I track my existing perfume order?

You can track your order in real-time from our Order Tracking page by entering your Order ID (e.g. #SCENT-10293) or phone number.

Are all perfumes 100% authentic?

Yes, unconditionally. Every fragrance and Oud extract at SCENT is formulated with 100% pure imported oils and tested for longevity, safety, and authenticity.

How long does delivery take?

Inside Dhaka delivery is typically completed within 24 to 48 hours. Nationwide deliveries outside Dhaka take 2 to 3 business days.

What is your return & exchange policy?

We offer a hassle-free 7-day return and exchange policy for any damaged, defective, or incorrect items received. Simply contact our support helpline.
